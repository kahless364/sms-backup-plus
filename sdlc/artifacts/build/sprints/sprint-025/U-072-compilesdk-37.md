# U-072: Raise compileSdkVersion to 37 (Build Only)

## Story
As the owner of this private, unpublished SMS Backup+ build (its only user; every device I use runs Android 11 or newer),
I want the level the app is compiled against raised from 35 to 37 (Android 17) and nothing else raised with it (`targetSdkVersion` stays 35), with lint run at the new level and its baseline refreshed in the open, the Robolectric default level kept at 29 for a recorded reason, and the continuous-integration workflow installing the matching platform,
So that every library and the later Thunderbird variants that need compileSdk 37 can be taken without waiting for the targetSdk 37 decision, and the app does exactly what it did before.

Source: story S3 of the approved design `DES-MODERNIZATION-014` (section "S3 - compileSdk 37", increments S3-I0 to S3-I4, the "Pre-specified move (N-6)", decisions D5, D6, D12 and D17, the gate matrix D18, rollback D19), which carries `REQ-MODERNIZATION-017` AC-2 (the compileSdk half), AC-13, AC-14, AC-15 and AC-16 for this raise. The requirement is as amended by CR-011 and CR-016 (Amendment 1; AC-15's count rule is by reference to `REQ-MODERNIZATION-015` AC-13). The orchestrator's rulings that bind the story are Records 13 to 16 of the appendix "Product owner decision" of `sdlc/artifacts/build/feasibility/U-062/thunderbird-mail-libraries-feasibility-analysis.md`: Record 13 row 4 (`compileSdkVersion` 37 does not wait for the one-time-code question), Record 14 (device instructions), Record 15 (item PO-A) and Record 16 ruling 22 (per-increment commits, by analogy).

Out of scope (each belongs to another story or is held on purpose):
- `targetSdkVersion` 35 to 37 and everything that comes with it: the behaviour-change review of levels 36 and 37, the Android 17 device validation, the one-time-code measurement and the BUG-021 fix: S5 (REQ-017 AC-1, the target half of AC-2, AC-3 to AC-12). This story leaves `targetSdkVersion 35` alone, so REQ-017 AC-2 is not closed by it.
- every toolchain and test-tooling version (Gradle, AGP, Kotlin, KSP, Hilt, androidx.hilt, Robolectric, Mockito, Truth, GreenMail, auto-service, JaCoCo, the CI action versions, the CI runner image): S2 (story U-071). Every runtime library (WorkManager, Lifecycle, coroutines, preference, annotation, core-role, security-crypto): S4 (story U-073). The minSdk floor: S1 (story U-068).
- `k9mail-vendored/build.gradle`: its `compileSdkVersion 35` and `minSdkVersion 21` stay (design D1 last rows, D4b; the module is deleted in Part B).
- raising the Robolectric default level, any test change, any dependency declaration, the SDK Build Tools version (36.0.0 stays: design D1), compileSdk minor levels 37.1 and 37.2 (REQ-017 names 37; design D12).
- publishing and rollout (the app is private; the Google Play target-level note does not apply).

## Bound decisions (recorded here so the plan does not re-decide them)

- `compileSdkVersion 37` only. The platform is `android-37.0` revision 2, the newest stable per research section 4.2 (the plan records the date on which revision 2 was confirmed as the newest stable platform 37 package, by the rule D1a 1); the levels 37.1 and 37.2 exist and are not used (design D12). `targetSdkVersion` stays 35; no commit of this story raises it (design D12; REQ-017 AC-5 co-landing rule is the reason).
- The DSL names stay `compileSdkVersion` and `targetSdkVersion`, so the requirement's grep works literally. If AGP 9.5 forces the newer `compileSdk` form, or resolves 37 to a platform other than `android-37.0`, the forced change is recorded with its error and the grep is adapted (design D12, A13).
- Lint: REQ-017 AC-14 option (a) (design D17): lint is run at the new compileSdk and `app/lint-baseline.xml` is refreshed, each newly baselined category listed. Findings in `NewApi`, `ExportedReceiver` or `ForegroundServiceType` are fixed, not baselined. Option (b), lint left red, is rejected by the design.
- Suppression allowed by the design in this story: baseline entries by option (a) in categories other than those three, and fixes at the site. The design allows no javac suppression (`-Werror` stays; no `-Xlint:-...`; a newly deprecated-for-removal platform API at 37 is fixed at its call site, never suppressed: D12, D3 javac rule), and names no `app/lint.xml` addition, no `@SuppressLint`, no `tools:ignore` and no `lint { disable }` addition for S3. If a finding can be neither fixed nor baselined within these rules, stop and report.
- Documents: the `compileSdk / targetSdk` row of `sdlc/artifacts/engagement/code-location.md` and `DEVELOPMENT.md:56` get one-line edits in S3-I3 (ruling on Scope Concern 3); `DEVELOPMENT.md:57` stays for the follow-up.
- Rollback is the whole story's commits reverted, newest first (AC-13).
- Robolectric: the default stays `sdk=29`; the comment in `app/src/test/resources/robolectric.properties` is corrected; the reason is recorded under REQ-017 AC-13; one probe at SDK 37 is run on a scratch copy of the tree outside the repository (not on a branch of this checkout; design D6 says "scratch branch" and the ruling on Scope Concern 7 overrides it), commits nothing, and leaves the working tree untouched (AC-8).
- The count rule is `REQ-MODERNIZATION-015` AC-13 as amended by CR-016: no number is hard-coded in this story or in a criterion (Record 15, ruling 14).
- Gates are `./gradlew :app:assembleDebug :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:jacocoTestCoverageVerification :app:assembleRelease` on JDK 25 as declared by `gradle/gradle-daemon-jvm.properties`. No JaCoCo floor is lowered; no `@Ignore` is added; `gradle/verification-metadata.xml` stays enforced.
- "Behavior preserved" (REQ-017 Constraints): raising compileSdk alone must not change runtime behaviour. It is shown by AC-4 and AC-5 (the packaged and merged manifests, the APK entry lists and the R8 output differ only as the compile level explains), by AC-10 (the same tests, the same totals, the same coverage inventory) and by AC-12 (no production file changes unless AC-3 requires a site fix, each listed).
- Device evidence: the design gives S3 an "optional smoke" only (D18); it is IC-3, run by the orchestrator, on `Pixel_9_Pro`, `install -r` only, no password handling, no setting change.

## Estimation Guidance

Complexity: low (design D2). The production change is one build line; the rest is a comment-only edit, one workflow step, one baseline refresh and a record. The weight is in the evidence: the baseline at 35, the manifest, APK and R8 comparisons that show nothing changed, the lint account entry by entry, and the probe. Expect about 12 to 15 Gradle invocations (baseline gates, the lint runs before and after the refresh, one gate run per increment, the probe run, the CI-parity run, the final gates) and, if the orchestrator chooses, one short device step. It fits one sprint as one story and is not split: the manifest and APK proofs, the lint refresh and the Robolectric record all concern the same one-line change (folding it into S5 would make compileSdk 37 wait on the one-time-code measurement and fix and on device permission though it changes no runtime behaviour: design D2, "Why five, and not fewer", item 2). Priority: high, because REQ-017 is high and S3 is a prerequisite of every REQ-019 Part B row that compiles Thunderbird code (design Overview).

Risks: (1) a javac warning from the 37 platform failing `:app` under `-Werror` (risk 13); (2) the lint refresh hiding more than it should, or a finding in a forbidden category needing a production fix (Scope Concern 5); (3) the N-6 move interleaving this story with S2 (Scope Concern 1); (4) the probe disturbing a shared checkout (Scope Concern 7); (5) an unexplained manifest, APK or R8 difference that says the raise was not behaviour-neutral (AC-4, AC-5); (6) CI never running (risk 12), which leaves AC-9 evidence local only.

# U-073: Raise the Runtime Libraries to Latest Stable, One Family at a Time

## Story
As the owner of this private, unpublished SMS Backup+ build (its only user; every device I use runs Android 11 or newer),
I want the runtime libraries the app is built on (core-role, annotation, preference, coroutines, Lifecycle and WorkManager) raised to their latest stable releases, one family at a time, and the libraries that are already current or cannot move (security-crypto, JUnit, jutf7) confirmed and left as they are,
So that no pre-release or years-old library carries the backup, restore, scheduling and default-SMS-role code into the Thunderbird migration, and each raise is shown (by the tests, by a re-run of the cancellation-check mutations, by the merged manifest, by the release build and by a run on a device) not to have changed what the app does.

Source: story S4 of the approved design `DES-MODERNIZATION-014` (section "S4 — Runtime libraries (REQ-016 AC-1 to AC-13)", decisions D1, D1a, D3, D5, D8, D10, D11, D16, D17, D18, D19, assumption A16, finding F-1). The requirement is `REQ-MODERNIZATION-016` as amended by CR-011 (minSdk 24), CR-016 (Context count; AC-6 states the configured floors, "from the commit recorded at the story's start", Amendment 1) and the retained-libraries exception of CR-010. The orchestrator's rulings on the order are Records 13 to 16 of the appendix "Product owner decision" of `sdlc/artifacts/build/feasibility/U-062/thunderbird-mail-libraries-feasibility-analysis.md`. This story carries the whole of S4: all of REQ-016 AC-1 to AC-13, no less, and nothing of S2, S3 or S5.

Every `HEAD c9770c30` reference and every line number in this story is dated context, read on 2026-11-21; line numbers are re-read at the story's start commit (`<start>`) and the names are what bind.

Out of scope (each belongs to another story or is held on purpose):
- the build toolchain and test tooling (Gradle, AGP, Kotlin, KSP, Hilt, androidx.hilt, Robolectric, Mockito, Truth, GreenMail, auto-service, JaCoCo, the CI actions) and the `junit:junit` row: story S2, U-071. S4 records JUnit's planning-date version and maintenance finding and does not own the row (design D1, "Consequences").
- `compileSdkVersion` 35 to 37: story S3, U-072. `targetSdkVersion` 35 to 37 and the behaviour-change review: story S5.
- the retained mail libraries (CR-010): `commons-io` 2.16.1, `apache-mime4j-core` and `apache-mime4j-dom` 0.8.11, `jzlib` 1.0.7 and `jutf7` 1.0.0 (`k9mail-vendored/build.gradle:100`, `:102`, `:107`, `:109`, `:111`) stay exactly as they are until the Thunderbird migration. The design's open item that Maven Central's newest `jzlib` is 1.1.3 goes to the Part B design, not here (design D1).
- the transitive libraries nobody declares: `com.google.crypto.tink:tink-android` 1.8.0 and `androidx.core:core` 1.12.0 are not overridden (design D1, second table).
- replacing the deprecated `androidx.security:security-crypto` (product-owner item PO-5, Record 15 item PO-F): a recommendation put to the owner by the orchestrator; it gates no story (design D11). This story records the finding and leaves the library at 1.1.0.
- the fixes of BUG-021, BUG-022 and BUG-023 (stories U-070, U-067, U-069: done before this story starts), the Android 17 measurement (S5-I1), and any change of behaviour on a supported level. Publishing and rollout (the app is private).

- **The `-Werror` rule applies to every increment** (design D10 "`-Werror`", D3): any javac warning a new library produces fails `:app`; record it verbatim, fix it at the call site, never remove `-Werror`, never add an `-Xlint:-...` suppression.

# U-068: Raise minSdk to 24 and Remove What the Floor Makes Dead

## Story
As the owner of this private, unpublished SMS Backup+ build (its only user; every device I use runs Android 11 or newer),
I want the app's minimum Android version raised from 5.0 (API 21) to 7.0 (API 24), and the code, manifest permissions, tests, lint entries and text that only served Android 5.0 to 6.0 removed or retargeted,
So that the later upgrade stories (Robolectric 4.18, WorkManager 2.12, and the Thunderbird mail libraries, all of which need API 24) start from a tree in which every statement about the supported levels is true, and no branch, test or permission describes a device that can no longer install the app.

Source: story S1 of the approved design `DES-MODERNIZATION-014` (section "S1 - minSdk 24 floor", decisions D4, D4a, D4b, D6, D8, D9, D17), which carries REQ-MODERNIZATION-015 AC-5 to AC-10, its gates AC-11 to AC-14 and the first part of AC-17 (a launch smoke; the full AC-17 run is story S2). The requirement is as amended by CR-011 and CR-016; the scheduler contract is `CNTR-MODERNIZATION-004` at v3 as set by CR-015. The orchestrator's rulings on S1's preconditions and order are Records 13 and 14 of the appendix "Product owner decision" of `sdlc/artifacts/build/feasibility/U-062/thunderbird-mail-libraries-feasibility-analysis.md`.

Out of scope (each belongs to another story or is held on purpose):
- every dependency and toolchain version (Gradle, AGP, Kotlin, KSP, Hilt, androidx.hilt, Robolectric, Mockito, Truth, GreenMail, auto-service, JaCoCo, CI actions): S2; WorkManager and the other runtime libraries: S4; `compileSdkVersion` 35 to 37: S3; `targetSdkVersion` 35 to 37: S5. The Robolectric default level (`sdk=29`) and the stale comment in `app/src/test/resources/robolectric.properties` stay as they are (D6; the comment is S3-I3).
- `k9mail-vendored`: its `minSdkVersion 21` and its five SDK-level guards stay (D4b; CR-009 moves the code later).
- the `SMS_RECEIVED` path (`SmsBroadcastReceiver.java`, its manifest entry), `BackupTriggerWorker.kt` (BUG-023), `BackupWorker.kt` and `RestoreWorker.kt` (BUG-022, story U-067).
- any change of behaviour on a supported level; publishing and rollout (the app is private).
